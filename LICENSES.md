This repository is a compilation of separately developed subsystem snapshots. **There is no single blanket license for the entire compilation.**

## Explicitly licensed material

The files under [`subsystems/fpga-mainboard/source/`](subsystems/fpga-mainboard/source/) were published with the GNU General Public License, version 3. The original license text is preserved at [`subsystems/fpga-mainboard/source/LICENSE`](subsystems/fpga-mainboard/source/LICENSE).

## Material with no explicit license found

At the imported commits, no `LICENSE`, `LICENCE`, `COPYING`, or `NOTICE` file was present in these source repositories:

- haptics;
- fingerprint sensor;
- flashlight;
- mechanical and thermal;
- ambient-light sensor; and
- IMU.

The additional light-sensor, real-time-clock, STM32-controller, and heat-sensor ZIP snapshots supplied directly for this consolidation likewise contained no archive entry named `LICENSE`, `LICENCE`, `COPYING`, or `NOTICE`. Individual third-party component files or generated source inside an archive may carry their own notices or license headers; those remain applicable.

Public availability on GitHub does not by itself grant a general license to copy, modify, or redistribute material. Rights remain with the applicable authors or rights holders unless they have granted permission elsewhere. Contact the original contributors or DIGITAL at Cal Poly Pomona before reuse beyond what applicable law permits.
