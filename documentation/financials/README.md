# Budget and financial records

`Budget and Financials .xlsx` is an eight-sheet working workbook that preserves:

- equipment and workshop purchase planning;
- submitted, final, and brainstorming Hatchery budgets;
- a PCB bill of materials;
- a combined project bill of materials;
- purchase tracking; and
- the main-board bill of materials.

The workbook includes formulas in its budget sheets and is retained unchanged as a historical planning record. It should not be treated as a current quote or purchasing authorization.
